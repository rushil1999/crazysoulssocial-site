Why we started this

Meeting someone in the Bay Area has quietly become the hardest easy thing there is.

Everyone is on the apps and nobody is enjoying them. The alternative is a loud bar on a Friday and the hope that something happens on its own, which it rarely does. We wanted a third option: an evening you are invited to rather than one you turn up at, where the awkward part has already been handled for you.

What makes it different

How an evening runs

Every other mixer in this city hands you a name tag and wishes you luck. Ours runs to a shape, and the shape is the reason people come back.

  1. i

    You arrive

    Doors open, the bar is open, and there is no rush. Come when it suits you.

  2. ii

    You tell us a little about yourself

    A couple of minutes, in your own words — what you are into, and the sort of person you would actually like to meet. Nothing formal, and nobody sees it but us.

  3. iii

    You settle in

    Have a drink, talk to whoever you like, let the room fill up. In the background we are working out who ought to meet whom.

  4. iv The part that matters

    We introduce you, by name

    Three or four introductions over the evening, each one chosen for you. We bring you across, tell you both why we thought you should meet, and then leave you to it.

This is not speed dating, and it is not a room of strangers in name tags.

Nothing runs on a timer, nobody rotates, and you never repeat your opening line to the whole room. The introductions are what you are paying for — the rest is a good bar on a good evening.

What guests said afterwards

We asked everyone how the San Jose evening went. Three things came back again and again.

It wasn't random

Before you reserve

Questions we get

Can I come alone?

Nearly everyone does, and the format assumes it. You are introduced to people by name over the course of the evening, so there is no point where you are standing on your own working up the nerve.

How are the introductions made?

From what you tell us about yourself on the night — a couple of minutes, in your own words. We work out who ought to meet whom and then make three or four introductions for you. It is done by hand, which is why these evenings stay small and why admission is by invitation.

Is this speed dating?

No. Nothing is on a timer and nobody rotates. You are introduced to a few people chosen for you and then left to talk for as long as you like, or to go and talk to someone else entirely.

Do I have to drink?

No. Every room we book has a proper non-alcoholic list, and a good number of people stay on mocktails all evening. Nobody notices, and nobody asks.

Who is in the room?

Working adults from across the Bay, generally mid-twenties through late thirties. Seats are held evenly on both sides so the room stays balanced.

Where exactly is it?

We name the quarter when seats open and send the address and arrival details to guests the week of.

Are photographs taken?

Yes, we bring a photographer. Say the word at the door and we will keep you out of all of it, no explanation needed.
